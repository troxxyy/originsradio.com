## Original Blender asset

- Editable scene: `assets/merch/no303-cap.blend` (packed textures and studio lighting).
- Web model: `public/merch/models/no303-cap.glb`.
- Studio fallback: `public/merch/no303-cap-campaign.png`.
- Reproducible model source: `scripts/build_merch_cap.py`.
- Cotton color and normal maps: `assets/merch/`.

The cap is built in Blender 5.0.1 using `public/merch/acid-bass-303.jpg` as the visual reference: six shaped panels, cotton shell and lining, a thin curved visor with six stitching rows, small yellow smile embroidery, eyelets, sweatband, and a plain blue adjustment strap. The visor projection is reduced from 0.91 to 0.64 model units; its bowed attachment follows the crown and the ends close at the temples. The smile now conforms to the crown at half its previous diameter. Invented side lettering, ivory tag and exposed silver buckle have been removed. Proportions are inferred from photographs; this is not a manufacturing specification.

Rebuild from the repository root:

```sh
/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender -b --factory-startup -t 4 --python scripts/build_merch_cap.py
```

Set `MERCH_SKIP_RENDER=1` to export the Blender scene and GLB without rendering. The GLB follows [Blender's glTF material and texture workflow](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/3.3/addons/import_export/scene_gltf2.html), with image-based cotton maps and material-grouped geometry. No third-party model or paid asset is required.

## Viewer

Three.js and its GLTF loader are loaded in a separate client bundle. The model has seven material groups; the final GLB is about 4.3 MiB. Front / side / back, drag, keyboard arrows, zoom, reset and optional rotation are available. The initial camera is lower and faces the same direction as the campaign portrait. Mobile vertical scrolling remains enabled over the canvas. The viewer renders on interaction; optional animation pauses when hidden or offscreen. GPU resources are disposed when navigating away, including models whose loading completes after unmount.

A Blender studio render replaces the interactive stage when WebGL cannot initialize, the GLB fails to load, or the graphics context is lost.
